
## Learning CMS

The Learn area is a structured knowledge system, not only a course-card page. Admins can create courses, modules and individual lessons. Lessons support written content blocks plus image/video/link resources. Public visibility is controlled by publish flags and course entitlement rules.

## Advertising slots

Advertising is an optional layer. All slots are disabled by default. Admins can enable specific placements (homepage, AI Analytics, Journal, Tools, Learn or Store), configure an AdSense publisher/slot ID, and constrain the presentation to a small bounded container. Ads must remain clearly identifiable as advertisements and must not imitate navigation or content controls.
